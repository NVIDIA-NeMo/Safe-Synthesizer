    evaluation_report_data

    evaluation_report_data ¶

    Evaluation results as structured data, which the HTML report is rendered from and other interfaces can present.

    schema_version is raised only when a field is removed or changes meaning; readers ignore unknown fields. A field added after a release needs a default, so data saved before it existed still loads.

    Classes:

    Name Description
    ReportDataModel

    Base of the report data models: ignores unknown fields so older readers accept newer data.
    MetricScore

    A metric's score and grade.
    Distribution

    Percentage of training and synthetic values in each category or bin.
    Points

    Points on the first two principal components.
    Projection

    Training and synthetic records projected onto the same two principal components.
    Metric

    A metric's score.
    CorrelationMatrices

    Pairwise column correlations, with rows and columns in the order of columns.
    CorrelationMetric

    Column correlation stability, with the correlations it compares.
    DeepStructureMetric

    Deep structure stability, with the principal component projection it compares.
    TextSemanticMetric

    Text semantic similarity, with each text column's embeddings projected onto two principal components.
    TextStructureDistributions

    Distributions of per-record text statistics in one column.
    TextStructureMetric

    Text structure similarity, with the statistics it compares.
    AttackOutcome

    How many membership inference attack evaluations got one grade.
    MembershipInferenceMetric

    Membership inference protection, with its attack outcomes and the columns left out of the attack.
    ColumnProtection

    Attribute inference protection of one column.
    AttributeInferenceMetric

    Attribute inference protection, with the protection of each column.
    PIIReplayEntry

    Replay counts for one PII type in one column.
    PIIReplayMetric

    How many training PII values reappear in the synthetic data.
    TimeSeriesMetric

    A time-series metric, with metric-specific details.
    GroupSelectionPolicy

    How groups were chosen for autocorrelation similarity.
    AutocorrelationEvaluationMode

    Whether autocorrelation is compared per group or over one global sequence.
    WindowEvaluationMode

    How much of window membership inference ran.
    SkippedComparison

    A group and column pair left out of the score.
    GroupSimilarity

    Mean similarity of the compared pairs in one group.
    ColumnSimilarity

    Mean similarity of the compared pairs in one column.
    AutocorrelationProfile

    Training and synthetic autocorrelation of one column in one group.
    AutocorrelationCounts

    How many groups, columns, and pairs were compared.
    GroupSelection

    How the evaluated groups were chosen from the shared groups.
    AutocorrelationDetails

    Autocorrelation profiles and summaries; null or empty where the metric stopped before them.
    AutocorrelationSimilarityMetric

    Autocorrelation similarity, with the profiles it compares.
    WindowCounts

    Windows per dataset, before and after sampling.
    ClosestMatch

    The synthetic window closest to any training window.
    WindowDistances

    Euclidean distance from each window to its nearest synthetic window, after standardizing each value.
    WindowAttack

    Membership inference attack results against the holdout.
    WindowGroupSummary

    Windows of one training group.
    WindowMembershipInferenceDetails

    Window counts, distances, copies, and attack results; null or empty where the metric stopped before them.
    WindowMembershipInferenceMetric

    Window membership inference protection, with its windows and attack results.
    Metrics

    Per-metric results. A metric is null when it is not part of the evaluation; a metric that was turned off or
    Scores

    Overall scores.
    DatasetCounts

    Row, column, and missing-value counts for the training and synthetic data.
    ColumnProfile

    Summary statistics of one column in one dataset.
    ColumnPIIStatistics

    PII detected in a training column and how it was replaced.
    ColumnResult

    Statistics and distribution stability of one training column.
    PatternSymbol

    One symbol of a replacement pattern and what it stands for.
    PIIDependency

    A column whose values a replaced column depends on.
    PIIReplacementColumn

    How one training column was replaced.
    PIIReplacement

    The PII replacement applied to the training data.
    DifferentialPrivacySettings

    Differential privacy settings of the training run.
    EvaluationReportData

    Evaluation results, including everything the HTML report shows.

    ReportDataModel pydantic-model ¶

    Bases: BaseModel

    Base of the report data models: ignores unknown fields so older readers accept newer data.

    Config:

    • extra: ignore

    MetricScore pydantic-model ¶

    Bases: ReportDataModel

    A metric's score and grade.

    Fields:

    • raw_score (float | None)
    • score (float | None)
    • grade (str)
    • notes (str | None)

    raw_score pydantic-field ¶

    Unscaled metric value, or null if it could not be computed.

    score pydantic-field ¶

    Score from 0 to 10, or null if it could not be computed.

    grade pydantic-field ¶

    Qualitative grade, such as 'Unavailable', 'Poor', 'Good', or 'Excellent'.

    notes pydantic-field ¶

    Warning or error about the score, or null.

    Distribution pydantic-model ¶

    Bases: ReportDataModel

    Percentage of training and synthetic values in each category or bin.

    Fields:

    • labels (list[str])
    • bin_edges (list[float] | None)
    • training_percent (list[float | None])
    • synthetic_percent (list[float | None])

    labels pydantic-field ¶

    Category names, with categories under 5 training records folded into one 'Other (N categories)' label ('Other (1 category)' for one), or bin labels such as '[0, 10)', or '3' for a one-value bin.

    bin_edges pydantic-field ¶

    For numeric values, the increasing bin edges, one more than the labels; null for categories.

    training_percent pydantic-field ¶

    Percentage of training values per label, or null where undefined.

    synthetic_percent pydantic-field ¶

    Percentage of synthetic values per label, or null where undefined.

    Points pydantic-model ¶

    Bases: ReportDataModel

    Points on the first two principal components.

    Fields:

    • x (list[float | None])
    • y (list[float | None])

    x pydantic-field ¶

    First principal component of each point, or null where undefined.

    y pydantic-field ¶

    Second principal component of each point, or null where undefined.

    Projection pydantic-model ¶

    Bases: ReportDataModel

    Training and synthetic records projected onto the same two principal components.

    Fields:

    • axes (list[str])
    • training (Points)
    • synthetic (Points)

    axes pydantic-field ¶

    Names of the two components, such as 'pc1', with any explained variance.

    training pydantic-field ¶

    Training records.

    synthetic pydantic-field ¶

    Synthetic records.

    Metric pydantic-model ¶

    Bases: ReportDataModel

    A metric's score.

    Fields:

    • score (MetricScore)

    score pydantic-field ¶

    The metric's score.

    CorrelationMatrices pydantic-model ¶

    Bases: ReportDataModel

    Pairwise column correlations, with rows and columns in the order of columns.

    Fields:

    • columns (list[str])
    • training (list[list[float | None]])
    • synthetic (list[list[float | None]])
    • difference (list[list[float | None]])

    columns pydantic-field ¶

    Column names.

    training pydantic-field ¶

    Training correlations, or null where undefined.

    synthetic pydantic-field ¶

    Synthetic correlations, or null where undefined.

    difference pydantic-field ¶

    Absolute difference of the two, or null where undefined.

    CorrelationMetric pydantic-model ¶

    Bases: Metric

    Column correlation stability, with the correlations it compares.

    Fields:

    • score (MetricScore)
    • correlations (CorrelationMatrices | None)
